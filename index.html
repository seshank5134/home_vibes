HomeVibes replaces traditional restaurant bottlenecks with local raw material vendors and step-by-step 4-step chef scripts. Built on a distributed cloud architecture featuring Supabase PostgreSQL, Realtime WebSockets, and Flutter Driver Telemetry.

Telemetry Map: Google Maps & Leaflet GPS
Currency: INR (₹) Standard
Mobile Driver: Flutter Dart 3
